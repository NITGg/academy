# US-AD-3-1: View Lessons and Attendance Reports

[← spec index](../README.md) · Area: Admin · **Status:** Spec

As an admin, I want to view lessons, teachers, students, attendance, and activity reports, so that I can monitor learning activity.

## Flow
1. 🔧 Open lesson & report management → ⚙️ display lessons/teachers/students/attendance
2. 🔧 Filter and open a record → ⚙️ display details and reports
3. 🔧 Export attendance or Flex reports as CSV or PDF

## Included features
- All lessons across courses
- Teachers & their lesson schedules
- Attendance history per participant
- Student info & login history
- Attendance reports for all live lessons
- Export attendance & Flex reports as CSV or PDF
- Per-lesson student attendance
- Overall attendance % per student across all lessons
- Per-course student activity report

- استعراض بيانات الطالب وسجل دخوله

- تصدير تقارير الحضور والكريدت بصيغة CSV أو PDF

## Notes
- Filter by course, teacher, student, date, lesson status, or attendance status.
- Exported reports follow the selected filters.
